Consultancy programmes

What is studied, and what it moves

Each programme is a defined study with a fixed scope and a written set of recommendations at the end. Programmes can be taken singly or combined into one visit.

Raw material preparation

Crushing / proportioning / mixing / balling
CBG

Coke breeze granulometry

A study of the entire coke crushing circuit. Recommendations follow from the measured results and are aimed at achieving optimum coke breeze granulometry.

T.IC/Breeze
10 working days
SME

Periodic and spatial mixing efficiency

A study of the proportioning section together with the working regime of the primary mixing drum. Areas of concern are identified and measures to counter them are proposed.

Mixing efficiencyQuality
10 working days
BAI

Balling index through moisture control

A study of the operating regime of the secondary mixing drum, along with the amount, place and method of water addition in the balling drum. Areas of concern are identified and corrective solutions provided.

Balling indexProductivity
10 working days

Charging the bed

Raw mix bunker / segregation plate / roll feeder
RZR

Reduction in rim zone effect

A study of the sinter mix profile in the raw mix bunker and of the horizontal bulk density distribution. Measures for reducing the rim zone effect are recommended from those findings.

Rim zone effectReturn sinter
15 working days
SSM

Segregation of sinter mix

The vertical segregation index and the vertical bulk density distribution are estimated. The angle of the segregation plate and the RPM of the roll feeder are then optimised for a higher segregation index and a proper vertical bulk density distribution.

Top layer yieldReturn sinterT.I
10 working days

Ignition and suction

Hood / windboxes / suction track
IGH

Optimisation of ignition regime

The temperature regime across zones, the free oxygen potential in each zone and the suction profile under the hood are all measured. Measures and modifications are then proposed to reach the proper ignition regime.

ProductivityGaseous fuel
10 working days
IGS

Under grate suction through aerodynamic mapping

An aerodynamic evaluation maps the entire suction track. Leakages are identified and quantified so that the maintenance team can prioritise its activities and plug them.

Under grate suctionProductivity
Phase I, 4 working days. Phase II, 10 working days

Discharge and yield

Pallet discharge / transport / screening
RSG

Reduction in return sinter generation

Areas of weak sinter production on the pallet are identified, along with fines generation during transportation. Measures to counter weak sinter formation and to cut fines generation in transport are recommended.

Return sinter
10 working days

The consultant

Kadiyala Srinivas

Mechanical engineer by training, sinter plant man by trade. Thirty three years on the shop floor, in erection bays and in operations.

Kadiyala Srinivas has spent his career in sinter plant commissioning and in the wider running of integrated steel plants. His work has been in large industrial projects, with the focus on operational efficiency and quality control.

He most recently served as Head of Operations at JSW Steel, Ballari, leading the combined operations of Sinter Plants 2, 3 and 4 with a production capacity of 10.58 MTPA.

His strengths are project management, erection and commissioning, mechanical supervision, raw material handling systems, sinter plant operations, team leadership, and quality control and testing.

He holds a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from Kolar, Karnataka, and has completed training in coke oven operating practice at Gaoping City, China, in iron ore characterisation at RDCIS Ranchi, and in ISO certification with DNV. He works in English, Hindi, Telugu and Kannada.
